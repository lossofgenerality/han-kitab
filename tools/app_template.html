Han Kitab

Islamic books written in Classical Chinese by Chinese Muslims, from Wang Daiyu’s Zhengjiao zhenquan (1642) to the late Qing: Islam explained in the language of the Confucian classics.

Read Liu Zhi’s life of the Prophet in Chinese beside Isaac Mason’s English, follow the history, browse the gallery of China’s mosques and Muslim quarters, and download every text.

About

Han Kitab · 漢克塔布 is a reader for Chinese Islamic classics and their translations, made by Computational Classnotes.

  • Texts: the 1921 text of Mason's The Arabian Prophet is in the public domain; this clear-text edition and the research report are © Computational Classnotes · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
  • Chinese originals: added as clear text when made from public-domain scans. Machine-read Chinese is marked as such until it has been proofread.
  • Offline: once opened, the app and its texts work without a connection. Ask needs one, unless it is answering from the texts.

Production: Principal Investigator Lucval · Oversight Directorate Zil · Editors Dara, Lucia, Zil · Software FreeFormer · Agent Kirari-chan (キラリちゃん). Programming: Hikari Noor Hoshino (星野ヌール).

Ask needs no key: anyone may ask. Answers are limited in length.
