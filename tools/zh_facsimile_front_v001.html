About this facsimile · 本影印本說明

The pages that follow are the original printed pages, reproduced as they are. Not a character has been changed, added or removed. The only work done on the images: isolated specks of dust under 7 pixels were removed, each page was trimmed to its printed area and centred on a page of one size. The original wording, punctuation, page order, the library marks and the binding pages are all kept.

以下各頁為原書印本，照原樣影印，一字未改。僅清除細小污點，裁去頁邊，置於同一尺寸頁面之中。

Work: 天方至聖實錄 (also 天方至聖實錄年譜), by 劉智 Liu Zhi (c. 1660–c. 1730), a life of the Prophet Muhammad in Classical Chinese, completed 1724. This volume holds the prefaces, the table of contents and juan 1 to 10 of the 20 juan.

Edition reproduced: printing of 民國十三年 (1924), publisher not stated, 295 pages. Copy in the National Library of China (中國國家圖書館), digitized and published on Wikimedia Commons as NLC416-13jh005373-59385 天方至聖實錄.pdf.

Copyright: Liu Zhi’s text (1724) and the 1924 printing are in the public domain. The original scan is kept unchanged in the project repository for public download. The cover, this page and the processing of the images are © Computational Classnotes · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

English: Isaac Mason translated a selection of this work as The Arabian Prophet (Shanghai, 1921); a clear-text edition is published alongside this facsimile.
